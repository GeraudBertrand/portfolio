La Lisière de R'yleh

An asymmetric Lovecraftian board game. A team of investigators cooperates to seal portals; one player is the Great Old One and plays to break their minds.

Read the rulesSee the balancing sheet

The game

Two sides with opposite goals. The investigators explore occult places and seal interdimensional portals, together. The Great Old One plays alone, with hidden cards, and wins when the humans lose their sanity.

What I designed

  • Sanity as the central resource: every risky action wears it down, bringing the player closer to defeat, or to forbidden powers.
  • Total asymmetry: hidden hand for the Old One, open cooperation for the investigators.
  • A board that changes as the Old One's horde opens portals.
  • The numbers on every Spawn and Action card, balanced in a spreadsheet so the tension never drops.

The rest of the company

Every visual is the work of Mélanie Velasco; Adrien Chateau built the physical prototype. The rules and the balancing sheet are linked at the top of the page.
