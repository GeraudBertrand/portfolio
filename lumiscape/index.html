Lumiscape

An asymmetric cooperative puzzle game in Unreal Engine. Two electric creatures must work together to escape an abandoned toy factory.

The game

Two players, two Lumiciels: creatures made of electricity, each with different powers. They move energy and light through the factory to solve puzzles that neither can solve alone.

What I made

  • Modular 3D assets for the industrial set, built to be reused and kept within a polygon budget.
  • PBR textures in Substance Painter, to give the place the look of old, worn toys.
  • Integration of the whole set in Unreal Engine, from Blender to the running game.

What I learned

  • The full art pipeline, from modelling to real-time integration.
  • Asymmetric co-op frustrates quickly: each player has to need the other, not wait for the other.
  • Polygon and texture budgets are decided early, or paid for late.
  • Keeping one visual direction across five people.
