Living Rock

A minimalist physics puzzle game made in 48 hours at UniJam 2023. Your companion is a living rock, and it crumbles if you walk too far from it.

Play it on itch.io

The constraint

The theme of the jam asked for distance to matter. We read it literally: distance becomes a direct threat to the character's companion.

The game

You solve physics puzzles by moving pieces of the room. The main piece is the living rock: useful everywhere, but if it strays too far from you, it falls apart.

  • Pressure plates that only the rock or an energy crate can hold down.
  • One-way conveyor belts.
  • Levers that keep doors open.

My part

With Robin Lefebvre, I designed the levels. Each room teaches a mechanic, asks you to use it, then adds a twist, so the difficulty climbs without a wall. In 48 hours, that meant deciding fast and cutting what did not fit.
