Accessibility

What this site does for accessibility, what it does not do yet, and how to tell me about a problem.

What is in place

  • Contrast: every text and background colour pair of the site reaches at least 4.5:1, the WCAG AA level for body text (measured on 14 November 2026).
  • Keyboard: every link and button can be reached with Tab, and the focused element is outlined in yellow. The Accessibility panel takes the focus when it opens and closes with Escape.
  • Motion: when your system asks for less motion (prefers-reduced-motion), animations, the opening sequence and the autoplay of videos are switched off.
  • Images: every image has a text alternative; decorative signs are hidden from screen readers, and the two drawings of the home page have a title.
  • Status of a project: it is written in words next to its mark, not shown by a shape alone.
  • Language: each page states its language, English at the root and French under /fr/.

The Accessibility panel

The Accessibility button in the menu opens a panel with three settings: text size (100, 120 or 140 %), a narrator that reads aloud what the pointer touches with the voices of your browser, and colour filters for protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia. The panel needs JavaScript; the rest of the site works without it.

Known limits

  • There is no “skip to content” link yet.
  • The site has not been audited by a third party.
  • The PDF documents linked from the Pot2Colle and La Lisière de R'yleh entries have not been checked for accessibility.
